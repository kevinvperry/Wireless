This dated reference preserves the curated city ordering used by the app. The complete raw research is held privately; use the live directory for current availability.

A dash means no separately branded city feed was identified in the researched catalogues. It does not mean an interstate or national stream cannot be heard in that city. Digital-only services are distinguished where identified.

## Coverage notes

- All eight ABC Local Radio capital-city services were found and decoded.
- SEN includes a Canberra racing feed; this is not labelled as a general SEN Canberra sports station.
- No separately branded local Hit feed was identified for Darwin; no separately branded local Triple M feed was identified for Canberra or Darwin.
- Nova city feeds were found for Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth. Smooth city feeds were found for those five cities, including the digital services.
- Gold and KIIS services retain their actual station brands and frequencies. Gold Adelaide digital is additionally available alongside GOLD1323 in the full directory.
- Hobart and Darwin have additional commercial and community stations in the full directory; the table covers the originally requested network brands.
- This table records catalogue coverage, not an ACMA licence audit or a claim of local terrestrial reception.
