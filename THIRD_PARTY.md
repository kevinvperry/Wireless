Rights remain with the respective owners. Wireless is an independent player; inclusion does not imply broadcaster endorsement.

The app fetches live audio directly from broadcaster endpoints. Availability, geographical restrictions and stream URLs may change. Source URLs and factual correction migrations provide provenance, not a blanket redistribution licence for source datasets or artwork.

Raw scraped responses, bulk research datasets, downloaded broadcaster artwork and production exports are excluded from new public source snapshots pending a separate rights/privacy review. Maintainers should review any proposed new asset or dataset before adding it.
