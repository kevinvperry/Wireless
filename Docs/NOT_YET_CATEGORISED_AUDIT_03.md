# Not Yet Categorised audit — batch 03

Audited on 15 October 2026. Scope: the next 30 published stations ordered alphabetically after batch 02.

## Outcome

- 28 active Australian online services retained and moved out of `Not yet categorised`.
- All 28 retained services assigned a controlled genre.
- Two records archived: one duplicate station and one finite field recording that was not a live radio service.
- All retained records received station-specific artwork and corrected network, location, description and website metadata.
- Stream probes tested endpoint response and audio delivery; they do not certify uninterrupted future availability.

## Research basis

Research prioritised station and operator websites, broadcaster-controlled streaming profiles and current stream response. Australian Broadcasting Media’s `abm21.com.au` operator page identifies an Australian broadcasting infrastructure business in Kotara, NSW; the related decade channels were audited as one Australian Digital Radio Network family.
