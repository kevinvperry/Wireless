# Commercial network genre audit — 14 October 2026

## Scope and result

All 253 published stations in Gold, Hit Network, KIIS, LiSTNR, Nova, Smooth and Triple M were reviewed. 248 music and soundscape services have a primary Discover genre. Five dedicated talk or sport services were reviewed and intentionally left without a music genre.

Official network-level sources were used for shared formats, with station pages and rova’s official directory used for exceptions. Existing non-empty descriptions and music tags were preserved.
