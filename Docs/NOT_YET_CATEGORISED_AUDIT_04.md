# Not Yet Categorised audit — batch 04

Audited on 15 October 2026. Scope: the next 30 published stations ordered alphabetically after batch 03.

## Outcome

- All 30 records verified as channels of the Australian Digital Radio Network and retained.
- All 30 assigned controlled primary genres, with secondary browse genres added where they improve discovery.
- All 30 received station-specific artwork and corrected network, description and official website metadata.
- Fourteen related channels from batch 03 were corrected from the operator infrastructure page to the network’s official public website and national service area.
- Stream probes tested endpoint response and audio delivery. The shared Icecast servers enforce a small connection limit, so direct audio, mount-limit responses, official network evidence and current independent monitoring were assessed together.
