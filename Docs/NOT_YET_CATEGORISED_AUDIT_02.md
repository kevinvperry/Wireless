# Not Yet Categorised audit — batch 02

Audited 15 October 2026. Scope: the next 30 published records ordered by station name after batch 01.

All preferred streams were re-requested. Each retained record was checked against an official station, network or regulator source. A responsive stream or third-party directory listing alone was not accepted as evidence of an Australian radio service.

## Outcome

- 21 Australian radio services retained, corrected and supplied with station-specific artwork.
- 18 retained services assigned a controlled genre; three speech-led religious or foreign-language services remain outside the Music genre lists.
- 9 records archived, including one duplicate consolidated into its canonical station.
- Current ACMA broadcasting licence details were stored where a current official licence record was identified.

## Genre assignments

- **Classic Hits:** 6iX, 7HO Classic Hits, 94.1FM Gold Coast.
- **Pop:** 91.9 Sea FM, 92.7 Mix FM.
- **R&B & Hip-Hop:** A.i.R.
- **Dance & Electronic:** 8-bit Remix Radio.
- **News:** 7RPH Print Radio Tasmania.
- **Eclectic:** 5THE FM, 7HO River, Plenty Valley FM, Gulf FM, 2GLF, 89.7FM Perth, 8CCC, 3WBC, TYGA FM and Alex FM.
- 89.7FM Perth and 8CCC also appear in **Australian Hits** because their official programming explicitly promotes local and Australian music. Mix FM also appears in **Classic Hits** alongside its primary Pop classification.
