# LiSTNR music genre audit — 14 October 2026

## Scope and result

All 105 published stations recorded under the LiSTNR network were checked against their official LiSTNR station pages. Wireless assigned a primary browsing genre to all 105 services: 101 music services and four environmental or steady-state sound services grouped as Soundscapes.

The audit added six controlled choices needed by this catalogue: Ambient & Wellness, Christian & Worship, Folk & Acoustic, Indie & Alternative, Musical Theatre and World Music. LiSTNR’s own genre labels remain as secondary music tags, while the primary Wireless genre keeps Discover concise.

## Soundscapes (4)

- [Cooling Fan (White Noise)](https://play.listnr.com/station/cooling-fan) — steady fan noise for sleep or focus.
- [Light Rain](https://play.listnr.com/station/light-rain) — rain sounds for relaxation and mindfulness.
- [Rainforest](https://play.listnr.com/station/rainforest) — nature sounds for meditation and unwinding.
- [Waterfall](https://play.listnr.com/station/waterfall) — flowing-water sounds for relaxation.

LiSTNR labels these services Ambient. Wireless groups them as Soundscapes so users can find sleep, focus and relaxation audio without mixing them into music-led Ambient & Wellness services. LiSTNR services such as Sleep, Focus, Lofi and Wind Down use ambient music or musical soundscapes and remain under Ambient & Wellness.

## Metadata and audit trail

The official station descriptions and genre labels were added where the directory fields were blank. Station names, streams, network membership and existing non-empty editorial fields were preserved. Each assignment has an optimistic version guard and a live `station.music-genre-audited` event containing the source URL and before/after values.

Preflight validation covered all 105 records and found no missing or duplicate decisions. The live database contains a primary assignment for every LiSTNR station, no assigned station lacks a description, and the foreign-key check is clean.
