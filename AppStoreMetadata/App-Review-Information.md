# Wireless 1.0 — App Review Information

WIRELESS 1.0 (BUILD 10) — AUSTRALIAN LIVE RADIO

No Wireless account, login, subscription or purchase is required. Internet access is required to download the station directory and play live broadcaster streams. Favourites, settings and the cached directory are stored locally. Wireless does not use iCloud synchronisation.

Wireless is a native iPhone and iPad app, also available on compatible Apple-silicon Macs as a Designed-for-iPad app. It provides more than 1,300 Australian metropolitan, regional, community, specialist and national radio services.

QUICK TEST PATH
1. On first launch, Wireless opens Discover because no station has been selected. Browse Networks, Locations, Music, News and Talkback, Sport, or Community & Specialist. Search can also find a station by name, network or location.
2. Open Networks or Locations > Melbourne and select a station. The main player opens and playback begins from the broadcaster’s stream.
3. Tap the heart to save the station. Open Favourites from the heart button, use Reorder, then choose another saved station. Favourites are stored on the device.
4. Switch between Playing, Details and Sync. Details shows the station’s available directory information.
5. Open Audio Controls from Playing. Where alternatives are published, select another stream. Vocal Boost offers Off, Light, Standard and Strong for compatible direct MP3/AAC streams.
6. Open Sync and enable “Sync audio to picture”. Wireless initially builds a buffer. When ready, adjust the delay to align radio commentary with television pictures. Availability and maximum delay depend on the stream. TV Sync and Vocal Boost cannot operate together.
7. Open the moon button. Sleep Timer pauses after a chosen duration. Sleep Schedule can repeat nightly, play a check-in chime and fade out if unanswered. Notification permission is requested only when this schedule is enabled.
8. Open Discover > Settings > Support for the complete in-app guide. Stream Diagnostics can be enabled from Settings and exported manually by the user.

LIVE STREAMS AND METADATA
Wireless connects directly to stream URLs supplied by radio broadcasters; it does not host, rebroadcast or download programmes for offline listening. Stream availability, regional restrictions, artwork, programme information and song metadata are controlled by broadcasters and may vary or be temporarily unavailable.

Where reliable metadata is available, playback surfaces show the song title, artist and station name. Metadata is available only with TV Sync off. Showing only the station name is expected when no usable current track is supplied.

CONTENT AND ADVERTISING
Live radio may contain broadcaster-supplied advertising or sponsorship. Wireless has no advertising SDK and inserts no developer advertising. It does not bypass DRM, access login-gated services or redistribute content.

BACKGROUND, NETWORK AND CARPLAY
Background audio supports playback while locked or backgrounded. Lock Screen, Control Centre, headphones, AirPlay and compatible vehicles provide controls. CarPlay uses the approved audio-app entitlement and offers the player, Favourites and station discovery.

Broad App Transport Security access is required because the Australian radio directory includes broadcaster-operated stream, playlist and artwork hosts, including legacy HTTP services.

PRIVACY, OPEN SOURCE AND DIAGNOSTICS
Wireless requires no account and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Diagnostic logs stay on the device until the user explicitly exports or shares them. Logs may contain station names, broadcaster-supplied metadata, stream URLs and timestamps; authentication values and device names are excluded.
