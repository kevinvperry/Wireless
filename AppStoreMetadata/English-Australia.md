## Description

Australia’s radio. One beautiful app.

Wireless is made with a genuine love for Australian radio. Discover more than 1,300 live stations from across metropolitan, regional and remote Australia, bringing the country’s voices, music, news, talkback and sport together in one thoughtfully designed player.

ALL THE MAJOR NETWORKS

Listen to stations from Australia’s major public and commercial radio networks alongside independent, community and specialist broadcasters. Browse networks alphabetically and move from national favourites to local voices without switching between different apps.

MUSIC FOR EVERY OCCASION

Find a soundtrack for every part of your day. Explore live stations by genre, including pop, rock, country, classical, jazz, dance, electronic, R&B, hip-hop, indie, alternative, folk, easy listening and more. Whether you are working, driving, relaxing, exercising or winding down, Wireless helps you find the right station.

EXPLORE AUSTRALIA

Browse national stations or discover radio by city, state, territory and region. Travel from major metropolitan broadcasters to regional and community stations, or search the entire directory for a particular station.

LIVE SPORT TV SYNC

Prefer radio commentary with the television picture? TV Sync can delay supported live radio streams so you can match the commentary to the action on screen. Adjust the saved delay until the sound and picture line up, then return to the game.

DESIGNED AROUND LISTENING

• Save and reorder favourite stations
• See available song titles and artists supplied by participating broadcasters
• Browse stations by network, location and music genre
• Use Vocal Boost to bring speech forward on supported streams
• Set a Sleep Schedule and let Wireless fade out when you drift off
• Listen through CarPlay, AirPlay, the Lock Screen and Control Centre
• Keep browsing the cached directory when the catalogue service is temporarily unavailable

Wireless is a native app for iPhone and iPad, with compatible Apple-silicon Mac support.

Stream availability, regional restrictions, metadata and TV Sync capability vary by broadcaster and stream format. Wireless connects to streams supplied by broadcasters and does not host or rebroadcast station audio. An internet connection is required for live listening.
